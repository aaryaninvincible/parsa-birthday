# 🌷 Parsa's Birthday Celebration Scrapbook 🎂

A handcrafted, interactive cottagecore birthday scrapbook website created with love and shared memories by **Aryan** for his best friend **Parsa**.

![Aesthetic](assets/stickers/tulips_bouquet.png)

## ✨ Features

- **🎁 3D Interactive Gift Box Unboxing**: Initial surprise overlay that unfolds with confetti and automatically begins playing their friendship anthem.
- **📷 Retro Digicam & Scrapbook Wall**: Vintage digital camera simulation with functioning shutter button, camera flash, LCD overlay, and photo reel, plus polaroid frames with washi tape pins and full-screen lightbox.
- **💌 Aryan's Heartfelt Letter (Interactive Envelope)**: Vintage kraft paper envelope with a cute checkered ribbon bow that unlocks and smoothly unfolds a personal handwritten letter.
- **🎵 Retro Vinyl Turntable & Music Station**: Spinning vinyl record player synchronized to their song ***Woh Din (Yaadein)*** by Arijit Singh (*Chhichhore*), complete with progress timeline, floating notes, and dedication note.
- **🎬 Parsa's Video Vault**: Retro TV player with custom tabs to switch between birthday reels and snapchat memory clips.
- **🌷 Interactive Tulip Garden**: Hand-drawn blooming tulips that reveal sweet compliments and friendship quotes upon tapping.
- **🎂 Make a Wish (Interactive Cake)**: Tap the glowing candle flame to blow it out and trigger a grand multi-stage confetti shower!
- **📱 100% Responsive**: Tailored for smooth viewing on mobile phones, tablets, and laptops.
